# Test case ZA 38: "Aufbrechen von Werkstück-Details"

This is the first real-world test for ManualCAD. The source is a textbook page, chapter "11 Schnittdarstellungen", page 71, "Vorlage zu ZA 38". The photo is `za38-aufbrechen-photo.jpg`, next to this file.

**The task:** redraw both parts on one A4 portrait sheet at 1:1, and show the details marked with a red dot (●) as **partial sections (break-outs)**. The red text, red dots and red layout dimensions (25, 30, 40, 15) are instructions to the student. They are not part of the finished drawing.

Every value below was read from the photo. Values marked *(est.)* are not dimensioned in the source and were scaled from the photo, so check them against the book before relying on them.

## Part 1: stepped shaft (one view, axis horizontal)

Coordinates are view-local. x runs along the axis from the left end face, and y = 0 is the axis.

|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 40 | ⌀28 | from the left face: counterbore (Senkung) ⌀20 × 17 deep; blind bore (Bohrung) ⌀13 × 28 deep with a 120° drill point |
| 40 | 52 | ⌀35 | |
| 52 | 70 | ⌀50 | collar; recess (Ausdrehung) ⌀42 × 10 deep, from x = 60 to 70 *(side est.)* |
| 70 | 76 | ⌀30 | neck *(position est.)* |
| 76 | 108 | ⌀24 | |
| 108 | 136 | ⌀15 | length 28 |

**Dimension chains:**
- From the left end: 40 and 52.
- From the right end: 28, 60 and 66.
- Overall: 136.
- Diameters: ⌀28, ⌀35, ⌀50, ⌀30, ⌀24, ⌀15.
- No diameter value sits on the part's centreline: every ⌀ value is slid along its dimension line (DIMLINEAR option `Text shift`), alternating ⌀28, ⌀30 and ⌀24 below the axis and ⌀35, ⌀50 and ⌀15 above it. The shift is 6 mm: it centres ⌀50 and ⌀24 in the ⌀24 half-section they stand in (⌀50's dimension line runs through it), 2 mm clear of the axis and of the outline; 8 mm put both values on the ⌀24 outline. ⌀15's dimension line lies beyond the end of the centre line, so it only moves 2,5 mm, which keeps it the text gap clear of its own extension line.

**Break-outs to draw (marked ●):**
1. **Left end, the counterbore and the blind bore.** The hidden edges become visible edges. Hatch the material between the bore and the outer contour, bounded by a freehand line.
2. **Collar, the recess ⌀42.** Break open the collar region around the recess, then hatch.

**Notes on leaders (ISO 128-22), taken from the Vorlage** — the cut details are dimensioned by note, not by a diameter dimension, because their surfaces sit inside the part:

| On | Note |
|---|---|
| Teil 1, counterbore in the left break-out | `Senkung ⌀20 x 17 tief` |
| Teil 1, blind bore in the left break-out | `Bohrung ⌀13 x 28 tief` |
| Teil 1, collar break-out | `Ausdrehung auf ⌀42 x 10 tief` |
| Teil 2, counterbore in its break-out | `Senkung ⌀20 x 8 tief` |
| Teil 2, milled recess | `Einfräsung 4 tief` |

The 120° drill point of the blind bore is drawn (the cone at the bore's end), not dimensioned — the Vorlage does the same. 120° is the drawing convention for a drill point; a 3D model of the part may use the real tool angle of 118° (the SolidWorks cross-check did), which changes neither the drawing nor any dimension.

**Where the notes stand.** The notes and their dimension values must not touch: every note, value and label stays inside the frame, and no line, arc or other text runs through it (`src/app/za38.test.ts` checks this on the plotted sheet).
- Teil 1: two rows between the shaft and the top frame line. `Bohrung` on the lower row; `Senkung` on the upper row, left of the top centring mark (sheet x 105); `Ausdrehung` on the upper row, right of it. The leaders are near-vertical, so none runs through the first letters of a note on a row it crosses.
- Teil 2: `Einfräsung` above the arm. The counterbore note has no room at 3,5 mm: above the part the 35 and the 30° take the space, and below it the note would cross the 62's extension line (sheet x 103) or the left centring mark (sheet y 148,5). So it is 2,5 mm (the ISO 3098 minimum on A4), left of its leader. The leader ends with its arrowhead on the counterbore wall and comes out through the ⌀14 hole without crossing an edge.

**No double dimensioning.** A depth that a note states is not dimensioned again: no `10` for the recess (`… x 10 tief`), and no `7` for the hole below the counterbore (15 − 8 is already fixed by the 15 and the `8 tief`).

## Part 2: fork lever (front view and top view)

Coordinates are view-local, with x from the left end.
- **Front view:** y = 0 is the bottom face.
- **Top view:** y = 0 is the symmetry axis.

**Front view:**
- **Left block:** height 15, from x = 0 to 35. Its top face is at y = 15.
- **Slope:** rises at 30° from the vertical, from (35, 15) to the top face y = 25 at x ≈ 40.8.
- **Right arm:** from y = 10 to 25, ending at x = 115. The step in the bottom face is at x = 53 (115 − 62), rising from y = 0 to 10.
- **Counterbore ⌀20 × 8 deep:** at x = 17 from the top face y = 15, with a through hole ⌀14 below it (hidden in the template).
- **Milled recess (Einfräsung):** 4 deep in the top face of the arm, from x = 74 (slot R11) to 115 (hidden in the template).

**Top view:**
- **Width:** 35 from x = 0 to about x = 85 *(est.)*, tapering to 30 at x = 115.
- **Hole:** ⌀14 through, with the counterbore ⌀20 concentric, centred at x = 17.
- **Slot:** 14 wide, with a semicircular end R7 centred at x = 85 (115 − 30), open to the right.
- **Recess around the slot:** 22 wide, with a semicircular end R11 that has the same centre.
- **Edges:** visible lines at x = 35 and ≈ 40.8 (the slope edges); a hidden line at x = 53 (the bottom step).

**Dimensions:**
- **Lengths:** 35, 30°, 62, 115, 17 and 30. The 30° arc has radius 10,5: its value stands outside, left of the vertical leg, and at a larger radius it runs into the 35's dimension line.
- **Heights and widths:** 15 (left block), 15 and 25 (right end), 35 (width), 14, 22 and 30 (slot, recess and end width). The 14's value is shifted 3,5 mm (`Text shift`) off the top view's centre line, which reaches into it.
- **Diameter:** ⌀14 on the hole, at 41°, its value inside the top view below the top edge.

**Break-outs to draw (marked ●):**
1. **Front view, the counterbore ⌀20 × 8 with the through hole ⌀14.** Break out around them, with visible edges and hatching.
2. **Front view, the milled recess 4 deep at the right arm.** Break out there, with visible edges and hatching.

| Need | Command(s) |

| 30° slope | DIMANGULAR (DAN) |
| Item numbers "1", "2" | TEXT (DT) |
| Cut-detail notes on leaders (ISO 128-22) | LEADER (LE), Terminator Arrow; height reset with an empty TEXT (DT) |
| Parts list | PARTSLIST |
| Title block | TITLEBLOCK (TB) |
| Output | PLOT → PDF at 1:1 |

## Pass criteria

- Every line type and width comes from its meaning:
  - visible edges are 0.5;
  - hatching, freehand lines, dimensions and centre lines are 0.25.
- Hatching sits only inside the cut material, at 45°. Hatch lines stop at the freehand line and at visible edges, and the bores stay free of hatching.
- No hidden lines are left inside a break-out (ISO 128-50).
- The PDF prints at true scale on A4: the 136 mm shaft measures 136 mm on paper.
- No line, arc or other text runs through a value, note or label, and all of them stay inside the frame (`src/app/za38.test.ts`).
- No value is dimensioned twice (a depth given in a note gets no dimension of its own).
